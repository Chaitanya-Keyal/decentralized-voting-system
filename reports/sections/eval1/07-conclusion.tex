\section{Conclusion}
% ===========================================================================

An election result is acted on at once and never reconciled, so a mis-stated
tally has no later event to expose it. Auditing the operator does not fix that.
It moves trust to the auditor, and to records the operator produced.

\projectname{} puts the rules that decide an election into contract code on a
public testbed: who may vote, when phases change, and which ballots count. A
reader who disbelieves the result recomputes it from the raw log. The nullifier
that stops a second ballot never reveals whose it was.

Anonymity rests on a Merkle commitment rather than Semaphore's on-chain group
management, which would charge a transaction and a leaf insertion per voter.
Only the root, the roll size and the leaf set's IPFS identifier reach the chain,
and the booth derives its own authentication path from the published leaf set.
The constant-cost claim is per operation, not per election: total gas still
grows with ballots cast, and the roll-sized work moved onto the registrar, the
auditors and the booth.

The non-guarantees in Section~\ref{sec:trust} stand. Eligibility rests on a
trusted setup this group did not run, one registrar decides who is enrolled, a
voter can be coerced into handing over their secret, and secrecy from the
operator holds only while the relayer runs separately from the registrar.

So \projectname{} removes the operator from the ballot box and from the count,
and leaves one party in control of the franchise.

Nothing here has been measured. Evaluation 2 replaces these expectations with
numbers against the parameters in Section~5. C1 measures registration and ballot
gas from ten voters to a hundred thousand; tier 3 should be flat, and above tier
2 throughout, which is the price of anonymity rather than a saving. C2 checks that
the work did not simply move to the browser: whether a hundred-thousand-leaf roll
can be rebuilt and proved on a phone. D4 needs no graph at all: with our backend
switched off mid-demonstration, the audit dashboard must still read the chain
and recompute the tally. Missed targets will be reported too.
