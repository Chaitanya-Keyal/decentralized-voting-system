\section{References}
% ===========================================================================

The contract layer follows two Ethereum standards. ERC-1167 defines the minimal
proxy that \texttt{ElectionFactory} clones for each election \cite{eip1167}.
ERC-2771 defines the trusted-forwarder convention that lets the relayer pay for
a ballot without becoming its sender \cite{erc2771}.

The anonymity scheme follows the Semaphore protocol: identity commitments,
membership proofs against a published root, and nullifiers \cite{semaphore}. We
use its circuits, verifier and nullifier scheme, but not its on-chain group
management. Access control, Merkle proofs, clones and the forwarder come from
OpenZeppelin Contracts 5.x \cite{openzeppelin}. The contracts target Solidity
0.8.28 \cite{solidity} and are built and gas-profiled with Foundry
\cite{foundry}.

The contracts deploy to Ethereum Sepolia, the recommended testnet for
application and contract development \cite{sepolia}. Published leaf sets and
candidate manifestos are addressed by content identifier on IPFS \cite{ipfs}.

The three client surfaces use the Next.js App Router \cite{nextjs} and reach the
chain through wagmi \cite{wagmi} and viem \cite{viem}.
